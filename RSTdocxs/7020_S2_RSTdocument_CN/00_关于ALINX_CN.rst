About ALINX
=============

ALINX Electronic Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., established in 2012 and headquartered in Lingang Science and Technology City, Songjiang, Shanghai, is a company specializing in automotive intelligent products and FPGA technology solutions. The company owns two brands, "AUMO" and "ALINX", where AUMO focuses on automotive intelligent products and ALINX focuses on FPGA products and custom solutions. After ten years of development, the company's products have been exported to over 30 countries worldwide.

- In 2018, the company passed the High-tech Enterprise certification and ISO9001 Quality Management System certification;
- In 2019, it was awarded the "Science and Technology Innovation Award" by Jiuting Town, Songjiang District, Shanghai;
- In 2020, it was awarded the title of "Specialized, Refined, Distinctive and Novel Enterprise" by Shanghai;
- In 2021, it was approved for the Shanghai Technology-based SME Technology Innovation Fund project, certified as a national-level technology-based SME, and passed the High-tech Enterprise certification review;
- In 2022, it received the special development fund from Shanghai Zhangjiang National Independent Innovation Demonstration Zone and was certified as a "Shanghai Innovative SME";

ALINX is an official partner of XILINX, the global leading FPGA chip company, and an official partner of Pango Micro, the domestic leading FPGA chip company. As a Baidu AI partner, ALINX has customized the Edgeboard series edge devices for Baidu's domestic AI PaddlePaddle system. Through years of R&D investment, ALINX has launched over 100 FPGA SoM modules and supporting boards, accumulated more than 1,000 enterprise batch users and tens of thousands of independent developers, with a far-leading market share.

Through its business model of products plus customized services, ALINX will continue to increase investment in product R&D and technology research, continuously cooperate with customers in vertical industries, focus on the high-end equipment field, and assist customers in solving technical challenges in their industries.
