RS422 Experiment
================

**The Vivado project for this experiment is "rs422_test".**

This chapter uses the AN3485 module to implement RS422 interface data transmission. The module has already been introduced in the previous RS232 experiment and will not be repeated here. The RS422 and RS232 interfaces to the FPGA are the same, both using TXD and RXD. Therefore, this experiment is based on the RS232 experiment, instantiating two channels connected to the RS422 interface chip MAX3490.

.. image:: images/15_media/image1.png
      
RS422 interface schematic

Program Design
--------------

The program design is relatively simple. Based on the RS232 experiment, simply instantiate two uart_test modules.

.. image:: images/15_media/image2.png
      
Experiment Testing
------------------

The RS422 interface uses differential signals with a total of four signal lines: two for transmitting TXD+ and TXD-, corresponding to Y and Z, and two for receiving RXD+ and RXD-, corresponding to A and B.

.. image:: images/15_media/image3.png
      
Similar to the RS232 experiment, a USB-to-serial device is used for connection. Use jumper wires to connect the module's RS422_1 Y and Z to the device's R+ and R- respectively, and connect the module's A and B to the device's T+ and T- respectively.
