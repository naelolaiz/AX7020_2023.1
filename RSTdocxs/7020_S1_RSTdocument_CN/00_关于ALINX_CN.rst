About ALINX
=============

Xinyi Electronic Technology (Shanghai) Co., Ltd., established in 2012, is headquartered in Lingang Science and Technology City, Songjiang, Shanghai. It is a company specializing in automotive intelligent products and FPGA technology solutions, owning two major brands: "AUMO" and "ALINX". The AUMO brand focuses on automotive intelligent products, while the ALINX brand focuses on FPGA products and customized solutions. After ten years of development, the company's products have been exported to more than 30 countries worldwide.

- In 2018, the company obtained High-tech Enterprise certification and passed ISO9001 Quality Management System certification;
- In 2019, the company was awarded the "Science and Technology Innovation Award" by Jiuting Town, Songjiang District, Shanghai;
- In 2020, the company was awarded the title of "Specialized, Refined, Distinctive and Innovative Enterprise" by Shanghai;
- In 2021, the company applied for and obtained the Shanghai Technology-based SME Technology Innovation Fund, was recognized as a National Technology-based SME, and passed the High-tech Enterprise certification re-examination;
- In 2022, the company received the Shanghai Zhangjiang National Independent Innovation Demonstration Zone Special Development Fund and passed the "Shanghai Innovative SME" certification;

ALINX is an official partner of the global FPGA chip leader — XILINX, an official partner of the domestic FPGA chip leader — Unisoc (PANGO), and as a Baidu AI partner, has customized the Edgeboard series edge devices based on the domestic AI PaddlePaddle system for Baidu. Through years of R&D investment, the company has launched more than 100 FPGA SoM modules and supporting boards, accumulated more than 1,000 enterprise customers, and tens of thousands of individual developers, with a far-leading market share.

Through the business model of products combined with customized services, ALINX will continue to increase investment in product development and technology research, continuously cooperate with vertical industry customers, focus on the high-end equipment field, and assist customers in solving technical challenges in the industry.
