Some may ask whether it is possible to learn ZYNQ with zero background knowledge. That depends on where the "zero" is. If you cannot even read a schematic, do not know what an array is in C language, or have no concept of pointers at all, that is actually a negative starting point. Learning ZYNQ requires basic hardware knowledge and proficiency in C programming.

This tutorial covers the FPGA part. Through continuous practice, you will master the basic workflow of FPGA development. Although this tutorial does not explain many theoretical principles in depth, practice makes perfect — the more you practice, the more you will gradually grasp the underlying concepts.
